<i>The palest ink is better than the best memory.</i> — Chinese proverb

<b>You remember what happened. Autumn remembers when.</b>

Most people keep the events and lose the order. Autumn is a private journal and personal archive: write a note into the right year, month and category, and find it again years later by when it happened.

<b>Capture</b>
* Pick a date, pick a category, type a title, and write. Back-date anything you forgot.
* Titles run to 252 characters, so a short note can be all title.
* Optional Quicknotes and a home-screen widget, for a line in passing.
* Import text and Markdown files, from the share sheet or a file manager.

<b>Look back</b>
* The Tree: the whole archive by year, month and category.
* The Year screen: highlights, personal growth, and any one category across a whole year.
* Search every note; results open at the matching line.
* Timeline: a life log built from memory, one year at a time, exported as a single web page.

<b>Private and offline</b>
* No internet permission. No account, no ads, no tracking.
* Notes are plain Markdown files in Documents/Autumn, readable without the app.
* Pictures are kept in one folder, out of your gallery.
* Back up the whole archive to a single zip.

Autumn needs access to your storage to keep its files in Documents/Autumn.
